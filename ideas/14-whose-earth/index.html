Whose Earth

When an Aster mission hands a payload to a Skyhook tether, both sides compute speeds and energies about the same planet, with different numbers for it. This page lists every line in either repository that states Earth's gravitational parameter, radius, standard gravity or rotation speed, recomputes Skyhook's canon on each program's Earth, and sizes every difference at the interface. It also reconciles the reboost energies Skyhook quotes for one throw.

At the K2 interface

The quantity the two programs would hand each other is energy: the tether's release C3 (v∞² for a hyperbolic throw) or a payload's arrival v∞. Going from Skyhook's Earth to Aster's, the shift splits into what μ does, what the radius does, and what happens when one program's speed meets the other's radius.
