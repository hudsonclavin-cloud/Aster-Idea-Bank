# Long Coast
> Fly idea 39's transfer through the real solar system and navigate it: an n-body propagator that matches JPL Horizons to 0.45–3.8 km over 678 days on six real asteroids finds that the chain's patched-conic Apophis departure, flown as handed on, misses by 10.1 million km and would cost 182 m/s at TCM-1, while re-aiming the injection costs 3.1 m/s at the periapsis (its v∞ moves 168 m/s, C3 0.511 → 0.580); with Orion EM-1's injection errors and Lucy's Deep Space Network tracking, five TCMs budget 116.6 m/s (99th percentiles summed) and deliver to ±18 km (3σ). A spacecraft is not an asteroid: JPL's own OSIRIS-APEX predict drifts 9,386 km from this model in 200 days until sunlight is added (0.0134 m²/kg fitted, then 424 km).

**Kind:** explorable tool
**For:** Aster

Tests from the bank root: `node --test ideas/40-long-coast/coast.test.mjs` (22 tests, about 20 s; two of them run ideas 38's and 39's committed kernels) and `node ideas/40-long-coast/ui-check.mjs` (56 checks in headless Chromium; `check.mjs` runs it for `tools/check.mjs`). ## What it is

Idea 40, module 10 of the twenty in *The Long Way Round* (ideas 31 to 50, launch to asteroid and back). Idea 39 handed on a transfer drawn as one Kepler arc about the Sun, from the Earth's centre to where Kepler puts the asteroid, with a refusal (`dynamics`) saying so. This module flies it and navigates it. You can:

- **choose the transfer**: six presets made by idea 39's kernel (Apophis on Aster's DLA-oracle cell, the chain's transfer; Bennu, Ryugu, Didymos, Eros and 2000 SG344 on idea 39's least-C3 reliable cells), or idea 39's handoff, re-derived by idea 39's own `parseHandoff`;
- **choose the departure and the aim**: idea 39's v∞ from Aster's 6,578 km parking orbit, idea 37's planned impulsive departure, or the state idea 38 flew when it closes; *re-aim the injection* (the v∞ that reaches the asteroid through every force) or *correct at TCM-1*; aim where the asteroid **really is** (Aster's elements integrated through the same forces) or where Kepler puts it;
- **switch forces** (the Moon, Mercury to Neptune, Earth's J2, the Sun's relativity) and set a **sunlit area per mass**;
- **schedule five TCMs**, and set **the tracking** (Lucy's assumptions or the DSN's data noise; passes and Delta-DOR sessions a week, data weights, the cutoff before each TCM, continuous tracking after launch and before arrival, unmodelled acceleration), **the injection accuracy** (Orion EM-1's ICPS: estimate, requirement, INCA) and **the execution errors** (Lucy's, EM-1's, none);
- **read** the re-aimed injection, TCM-1 for the departure as given, the uncorrected miss, Kepler's offset, the TCM budget (deterministic, mean, 99th percentile), the delivery and knowledge at arrival (3σ), the arrival v∞ and the tracking used, each with a confidence chip, and every refusal; **watch** the uncertainty grow and be cut back, the budget per TCM, the path from the ecliptic pole and the arrival ellipses;
- **check it against JPL**: six asteroids over 678 days from Horizons states and from Aster's elements, Apophis through its 2029 encounter, the Moon's fit, and a sunlight fit on JPL's predict of OSIRIS-APEX (the spacecraft that will reach Apophis in 2029);
- **export the handoff** for module 41 (copy, download, link).

Idea 38's `post_cutoff` refusal asks for the Moon and the Sun on the way out ("module 40").
- **Aster's own items it serves** (BRIEFING §1, `strategy/ASTER_ROADMAP_2026-08.md`): *22 Gravity*, "frame-agnostic Cowell propagator with perturbation callbacks, DP5(4) + DOP853 class, GMAT/poliastro closure oracles" (this is a DP5(4) Cowell propagator with a Horizons closure oracle); *20 Mission View*, "animated transfer arc with uncertainty envelope" (the uncertainty envelope is computed here); the house rule that a number that cannot be trusted is refused, not shown.
- **What a person decides with it.** How much propellant to carry for corrections, and where it goes (TCM-1 is 95 % of it here); whether to buy Delta-DOR (without it the delivery is 4.3 times wider); when to put TCM-1 (10 days: 110 m/s at 99 %; 30 days: 146 m/s; 3 days: refused, nothing has been tracked yet); how good the upper stage's injection has to be (EM-1's requirement saves 26 m/s against its estimate); whether a porkchop v∞ can be flown as given (no: 182 m/s at TCM-1, against 3.1 m/s at the periapsis).

Alternatives weighed. (1) *A two-body coast with a TCM sized from the Lambert re-aim* (idea 39 did that for idea 38's departure: 3.3 m/s). Rejected: the question is what the Sun, Earth and Moon do to a low-energy departure, and two-body cannot answer it (the answer here is fifty times that). (2) *Port poliastro's or Aster's propagators.* Aster has none with perturbations yet (Slice 22 is planned), and INV-024 makes poliastro an oracle, not a source; JPL Horizons is the stronger oracle anyway, because it is the real integration. (3) *A Monte Carlo navigation simulation with a filter run on simulated data.* Heavier, and noisier at a few hundred runs; **built: a linear covariance analysis** (as Orion EM-1's, AAS 15-758, and JPL's statistical maneuver analyses), checked against a nonlinear Monte Carlo of TCM-1 and against closed forms. (4) For the target, *Aster's two-body ephemeris* (what idea 39 used) or *JPL's Horizons ephemeris for each body*: rejected, the first is 75,000–822,000 km off by 2028 and the second covers only the bodies fetched; **built: Aster's own elements integrated through the same forces**, which come within 0.5–6.9 km of Horizons.

## How it works

**Forces and integrator** (`coast.js` `accel()`, `dopri()`, `integrate()`). Dormand–Prince 5(4) (Dormand and Prince, *J. Comp. Appl. Math.* 6, 1980), adaptive, relative tolerance 1e-12, with the 6×6 state transition matrix (STM) integrated from the gravity gradient (point masses and Earth's J2; relativity and sunlight left out of it). Heliocentric ICRF/J2000 axes, TDB, as idea 39 and Aster. The Sun (GM 1.32712440018e20 m³/s², Aster's `keplerian.ts`), the Earth (398,600.4418 km³/s², Aster's `delta-v.ts`; J2 1.0826e-3 and radius 6,378.1366 km from DE440's header, pole along ICRF z), the Moon and the Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune systems (GMs from JPL's DE440 header, `snapshots/de440-constants.js`), each with its indirect term (the Sun's own pull toward it), and the Sun's Schwarzschild term with PPN β = γ = 1 (c from the same header). Sunlight when an area per mass is given: Cr (A/m) L☉/(4πcr²), L☉ = 382.8e24 W from NASA's Sun fact sheet, no eclipses. Within 1 million km of the Earth the state is integrated relative to it, against the Earth ephemeris' own acceleration (idea 39's Chebyshev velocity differenced inside one segment): the same problem, but the heliocentric form loses about 1e-5 m/s an hour to round-off near the periapsis (positions of 1.5e11 m carry 3e-5 m of it); with the switch, a departure-to-arrival propagation agrees with itself to 4–43 m across tolerances 1e-11 to 1e-14, where the heliocentric form scattered by 4–19 km.

**Ephemerides.** The Earth: idea 39's Chebyshev fit of Aster's daily Horizons file, through idea 39's own evaluator. The Moon (about the Earth), Mercury, Venus and Mars: Chebyshev series fitted here to Aster's same daily file (`src/v2/data/horizons-inner-solar-system-2026-2040.json`, 62ddb17): the Moon on 32-day segments, degree 20, whole kilometres, 3.4 km and 3.8 km worst against hourly Horizons in two fetched 30-day windows (2026, 2035); Mercury 64-day, degree 18, 100 km units (707 km worst at a record); Venus and Mars 128-day, degree 16, 10 km (31 and 29 km). Jupiter to Neptune barycentres: fitted to Horizons records every 4 days, 2026–2041, 256-day segments, degree 10, 10 km (24 to 29 km worst at a record). Each matters: dropping Mercury moves Apophis 274 km over the 678 days, Venus 32,826 km, Jupiter 8,168 km, Uranus 30 km, Neptune 7 km. Coefficients are written base 36 to keep the folder small.

**The departure** (`departureOptions()`). *Patched conic*: the hyperbola from Aster's 6,578.137 km parking radius whose outgoing asymptote is idea 39's v∞, in the least-inclined plane through it (inclination = |DLA|), periapsis at idea 39's departure epoch. *Planned (idea 37)*: idea 37's departure point plus its impulsive Δv (offered when idea 37's asymptote is the transfer's). *Flown (idea 38)*: idea 38's cutoff state when it departs.

**The target** (`targetState()`). Aster's elements (epoch 2026-05-01, from idea 39's excerpt) turned into a state by idea 39's own Kepler (Aster's `keplerian.ts` formulas) and integrated through the same forces; no Yarkovsky, no asteroid perturbers. Or, for comparison, idea 39's two-body position.

**The aim** (`aimVinf()`, `aim()`). *Re-aim the injection*: Newton on the v∞ (three unknowns, three miss components at idea 39's arrival epoch), its Jacobian the STM's position rows times the derivative of the analytic hyperbola, with backtracking, to 1 km (the floor of the integrator's own noise from a periapsis, where 1 cm/s moves the arrival 300 km); started from idea 39's v∞ or from it plus the TCM-1 correction, whichever misses less. *Correct at TCM-1*: fixed-time position targeting from TCM-1's node, Newton on the STM, to 1 m. A re-aim that moves v∞ by more than half of it is flagged (`patched_conic`): the n-body transfer found may not be the cell idea 39 chose. The reference cruise is then integrated node by node (every 2 hours) with the STM restarted at each node; a return inside the Earth's sphere of influence after leaving it is refused (`cruise_earth_encounter`).

**The navigation analysis** (`navigate()`). Linear covariance analysis (P. Maybeck, *Stochastic Models, Estimation and Control* I, 1979, as used by Zanetti and D'Souza for Orion EM-1, AAS 15-758): the estimate's covariance P_est and the knowledge error's P_nav (their sum the trajectory's dispersion) carried by the STMs; unmodelled acceleration as white process noise matched to Lucy's 1-day batches (q = σ²·1 day, σ 1e-9 m/s²); momentum dumps every 4 days (0.4 mm/s 3σ per axis, Lucy). The injection error starts P_nav: Orion EM-1's ICPS accuracy at TLI, 3σ in radial, along and cross (Table 2: 9.4, 59.7, 3.3 km and 52.1, 8.9, 13.0 m/s; Table 9 for the requirement and INCA cases), diagonal, at the departure state. **Measurements**: DSS-14, DSS-43 and DSS-63 (810-005 module 301 Rev. P, Table 2, ITRF93), turned by the Earth rotation angle (ERFA `era00` constants, idea 37's snapshot; UT1 = UTC; no precession, nutation or polar motion; no light time), above 10° elevation (module 301's low-power transmit limit). Two-way Doppler at every node of a pass, one range point per pass, and Delta-DOR (the geometric delay B·û, in angle over the baseline's projection on the plane of the sky) once per baseline per session (Goldstone–Canberra, Goldstone–Madrid, both antennas above 10°), each a scalar Kalman update. Lucy's preset (AAS 17-632 Table 6, the assumptions for the 60 days of each Trojan approach, applied here to the whole cruise): Doppler 0.1 mm/s, range 100 m, Delta-DOR 0.2 ns; three 8-hour passes and two Delta-DOR sessions a week; data cut off 3 days before each TCM; continuous tracking for 10 days before arrival (and here for 7 after launch). DSN preset: Doppler 0.03 mm/s (DESCANSO Monograph 1, Table 3-3, X-band 60 s), range 2 m (its instrument bias, one point a pass), Delta-DOR 0.057 ns (810-005 module 210 Rev. F, Table 8, random and systematic). **TCMs**: each nulls the estimated miss at the arrival epoch, Δv = G x̂ with G = [−Ψ_rv⁻¹Ψ_rr, −I] from the STM to arrival Ψ; its Δv is N(deterministic, G P_est Gᵀ), whose mean, 99th and 99.73rd percentiles come from 4,000 seeded samples; afterwards P_est ← (I+BG) P_est (I+BG)ᵀ and P_nav gains the execution error. A TCM designed before any tracking datum refuses the budget (`tcm_schedule`: there is no estimate to correct); one with no datum since the previous TCM is flagged, and its statistical Δv is zero. **Execution errors**: Gates' four-term model (JPL TR 32-504, 1963: shutoff and resolution along the burn, pointing and autopilot across it), with Lucy's "30 mm/s + 4 %, 3σ" as spherical fixed and proportional terms, or EM-1's Table 6 (noise and bias 3 mm/s, scale factor 30 ppm, misalignment 0.03°, 3σ). The budget sums the per-TCM 99th percentiles, as EM-1's Table 7 totals its 99.73 % values. The delivery and the knowledge at arrival are projected on the approach plane across the arrival v∞ (S), T in the ecliptic.
