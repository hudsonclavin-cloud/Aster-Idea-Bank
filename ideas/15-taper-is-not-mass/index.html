Taper is not mass

EQ-5 gives how much thicker the tether is at its centre than at its tip. EQ-6 gives how heavy the whole tether is compared with the payload. The program once used the first as if it were the second. Both depend on one number, x = (vtip/Vc)·√(SF/(1 − derate)), and they differ by exactly 2√π·x·erf(x).

Quoted verbatim from docs/DECISION_LOG.md and docs/glossary.md (snapshots). A quoted figure is exact when the computation rounds to it at the precision it was written with, close when it is within 5 %, and otherwise does not reproduce.

    What is computed, and from what

    • EQ-4 Vc = √(2σ/ρ), from the fibre's strength and density in params/rev_a.json. Never the file's rounded V_c_kms: the ladder only reproduces from σ and ρ.
    • EQ-5 taper = exp(x²), the ratio of the peak cross-section (at the centre) to the tip's. With no derate this is taper_ratio() in physics/skyhook_physics.py; with a derate it is the glossary's convention, the strength reduced by the same factor as in EQ-6.
    • EQ-6 M/m = 2√π·x·exp(x²)·erf(x), the Moravec/Forward integrated two-arm tether-to-payload mass ratio.
    • Both come from one constant-stress arm whose cross-section grows as exp(x²(1 − u²)) from the tip (u = 1) to the centre (u = 0). EQ-5 is its peak; EQ-6 is 2 arms × 2x² × its mean. So M/m ÷ taper = 2√π·x·erf(x), a function of x alone.
    • Tier pins, the ladder, the payload used for tonnes, the safety factor and the guardrail are read from the canon file at load. The ladder's derates other than the conservative one appear in that file only inside key names (such as baseline_15pct_derate), and the page reads them from there.
    • "Custom fibre" is not canon (Rule 1): its curves are dashed and marked so. A fibre whose density the canon does not give is refused, not guessed.
