Hole Stack

A fifth selector for PartForge. Pick a metric size, a head and what holds the far end; the page works the whole hole out of PartForge's own fasteners_metric.json and fits.json: clearance hole with its ISO 286 band, counterbore or countersink, nut pocket, tapped hole. Every number keeps the table's confidence for that field. FLIGHT rows are cited only when every field behind them is likely or certain with a named standard; a recall number is shown, never cited; what the tables cannot answer is refused by name.

ISO 273 clearance
Far end
Curve tolerance

Written as the first OpenSCAD parameter and as the FLIGHT block's tess_tol_mm. 0.01 is what every PartForge flight template carries.

      OpenSCAD

      A cutter module to difference() from your plate, with $fn=fn_tol(d) on every round primitive and a comment on every dimension naming the table field, the standard and the field's confidence. Not compiled here: OpenSCAD is not available to this page.

      
  
      

  
      
    
      FLIGHT rows
      
      
      
    
      Rows to merge into the part's own FLIGHT block. FLIGHT tolerances are symmetric (d_mm ± tol_mm), so an H band +IT/0 is written as its centre ± IT/2, and a feature the table gives only a minimum for is written as that minimum + the process's default tolerance, centred, as tol_src: "default".
