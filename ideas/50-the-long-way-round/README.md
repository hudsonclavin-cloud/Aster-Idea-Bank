# The Long Way Round
> The whole mission, flown: ideas 31 to 49 run in order on their own committed kernels for 35 choices of rocket, propellant and asteroid. On the chain's own choices it reproduces all 19 committed handoffs and finds the rocket 12.8 t of propellant short of leaving Earth orbit (a refusal the committed chain loses at idea 41) and the capsule aimed 67 km too high to enter, so the 9.5 t of Apophis it lands is on paper; only Saturn V carrying a 2.26 t spacecraft departs, and with its way home aimed at the entry it closes once the tanks hold 0.06 kg more for the touch-and-go. Its round trips match JPL NHATS's total Δv to a median 4.0 m/s (0.04 %).

**Kind:** explorable tool + CLI
**For:** Aster
**Run:** open `ideas/50-the-long-way-round/index.html` in a browser (classic scripts, `file://` works, no network; it also opens on a `#h=` link carrying any handoff of ideas 31 to 50). Fly a mission yourself from the bank root: `node ideas/50-the-long-way-round/run-chain.mjs --rocket as512-sc --asteroid 99942` (about 20 s; `--help` lists the rockets, propellants and asteroids, `--handoff FILE` writes the mission's handoff, `--verify` re-runs the chain against the 19 committed samples). Tests: `node --test ideas/50-the-long-way-round/mission.test.mjs` (13 tests, about 55 s: two of them fly the chain whole) and `node ideas/50-the-long-way-round/ui-check.mjs` (92 checks in headless Chromium; `check.mjs` runs it for `tools/check.mjs`). Regeneration, none needed to run anything: `run-chain.mjs --build` (every mission, about 7 minutes), `--nhats`, `--sample`; `node ideas/50-the-long-way-round/build-data.mjs --aster | --reuse | --nhats <dir>` after `bash ideas/50-the-long-way-round/oracle/fetch-sources.sh <dir>`.

## What it is

Idea 50 is the last of the twenty modules of *The Long Way Round* (ideas 31 to 50, launch to asteroid and back). Each earlier module simulates one stage and hands one JSON handoff to the next. This one flies them all, in order, and makes a mission out of what comes back.

- **Pick a rocket, a propellant and an asteroid.** Rockets: idea 34's sample stack (seven of the engine ideas 31 to 33 build from the propellant you pick, one J-2, 10 t of payload), the same stack carrying only the spacecraft (its payload closed on the launch mass idea 42 sizes, and its way home aimed at the entry idea 48 flies), Saturn V AS-512 as flown with its Apollo stack, AS-512 departing with the spacecraft instead (closed the same way), or the chain exactly as committed. Propellants: nine of idea 31's presets. Asteroids: the six bodies ideas 40 and 47 can fly to (Apophis, Bennu, Ryugu, Didymos, Eros, 2000 SG344). Each of the 35 combinations offered is a mission `run-chain.mjs` flew whole on the committed kernels; a combination it did not fly is refused with the command that flies it.
- **Read the verdict.** *Closes* only if the chain reaches the ground with every link present and no seam breaks; otherwise *lands, open* with what breaks, *does not reach the ground* (an import that ends early), or *stops at idea N* with that module's refusal.
- **Four charts that follow the choices:** mass from the pad to the ground (nine events, log scale, with the what-if dashed over it); delta-v burn by burn on a log axis, coloured by who pays (rocket, spacecraft, return craft) and hatched where the link says the propellant was not there, with the total as one stacked bar; the timeline from launch to landing with the cruise, stay, mining, way home and the span idea 41 planned a radio link for; payload against C3 for Aster's eight NASA LSP launch-vehicle curves with this mission's C3, the spacecraft's launch mass and the flown rocket's capability on it. Tap or hover any mark for its value and the handoff path it came from.
- **Close it yourself.** Change the spacecraft's dry mass, the touchdown speed and the launcher; launch the propellant plant's coolers from the Earth; size the tanks for the touch-and-go. The page re-runs the chain's own mass budgets (idea 42's tank sizing, idea 47's return ledger copied verbatim, idea 49's main parachute) with everything else held, and reports the launch margin, whether the touch-and-go is paid, the material that comes home, the entry and landed masses and the main parachute.
- **Seams, refusals, every mission, reality, links.** Where each link starts from something other than what the last one ended with, measured; all the refusals the chain raised, with which ones a later module answers and which ones never reach the end; a matrix of all 36 missions; JPL NHATS's round trips flown through the chain; the 19 links with their digests and pages; import and export of the handoff.

## Why I made it

- **The series asks for it.** `queue/SERIES_LONG_WAY_ROUND.md`, row 50: "The whole mission, chained: one page that runs 31–49 in sequence with a mass ledger and a Δv waterfall, a timeline, and every refusal carried through", taking "a rocket, a propellant, an asteroid". Idea 49's README (*Where it would land*): "Module 50 takes `mass.landed_kg`, `loads`, `landing` and `timeline` for the mission ledger and timeline, and carries the `tps_thickness`, `landing_site` and `parachutes` refusals to the end." This page takes those and the 18 handoffs before them.
- **Aster asked for honest end-to-end numbers.** Aster's porkchop prices a sample return's delta-v stack and reads a launch vehicle's payload at C3 (`src/v2/porkchop/delta-v.ts`, `launch-vehicles.ts` at `62ddb17`) but nothing in Aster flies the mission through: no spacecraft is sized for an arrival, no launcher is checked against that spacecraft, nothing comes home. The chain did all of that in pieces; nobody had put the pieces end to end and asked whether they agree.
- **What a person decides with it.** Whether a rocket can send this spacecraft to this asteroid (and by how much, against eight real launchers); what the spacecraft weighs once it has to carry its own plant and the touch-and-go; how much comes home for each kilogram of dry mass; which asteroid the chain can actually reach and where it stops for the others; and which links of the chain are the weak ones (the seams).

Alternatives weighed.
1. *Run the 19 kernels in the page.* Rejected: the 228 files the runner loads from them are 5.2 MB (1.3 MB of kernels and loaders, 3.8 MB of snapshots: Chebyshev ephemerides, shape models, thermochemistry, earlier kernels' copies) in their own folders, against this folder's 650 KB limit, and copying them would make a second copy of every module. Built instead: `run-chain.mjs`, which drives each module's committed kernel where it lives (its own loader, its own sample recipe), pins every file it loaded by git blob id, and writes what the ledger needs; the page reads that, and a test re-flies two missions and fails if the bundle or any kernel has drifted.
2. *Compose the committed sample handoffs only.* Rejected as the whole answer (kept as one of the choices): every sample was built on its own assumptions, so their composition is the chain as it happens to be, with idea 41 rebuilt from a preset, not a mission anyone chose.
3. *A mission-level mass model of my own.* Rejected: it would be a second implementation of ideas 42, 47 and 49. The what-if re-runs their own budgets (47's functions copied verbatim) and is tested to reproduce their numbers to 1e-8.
4. *An end-to-end Apollo 17 check from the pad to translunar injection.* Considered as the reality oracle; idea 37 refuses a departure point for a C3 below zero, so the chain cannot fly it without a new path through idea 38, and ideas 36 and 38 already compare their own pieces with the flight report. Built instead: JPL NHATS round trips, which test this module's own composition (two legs from two modules summed into one total).

## How it works

**The runner** (`run-chain.mjs`). Each module through its own loader, in order, each fed the handoff the previous one just made: idea 31 by the calls its page makes (`rocket()`, `sideDensity()`, `stoichiometricOF()`, `makeHandoff()`, the two assumption sentences of its `app.js`), ideas 32 to 40 by the `sample()` their `build-sample.mjs` exports, ideas 41 to 49 by the import, run and `buildHandoff` calls their build scripts make. Nothing is re-implemented. With the chain's own choices it reproduces all 19 committed handoffs' outputs (see Tests). The choices:
- *Propellant:* idea 31 at 10 MPa (the chain's) and the O/F its page opens a preset at (the c\* peak in the preset's checked range, to 0.001), except LOX/RP-1 at the chain's 2.6.
- *Rocket:* idea 34's sample recipe with that engine; `chain-sc` sets its payload to idea 42's launch mass; `as512` is idea 36's own AS-512 set-up (`C.setup(DATA.as512)`, guided, 100-flight Monte Carlo at seed 1, as its page does); `as512-sc` is the same with idea 38's `payloadKg` setting at idea 42's launch mass (idea 38 does not re-fly the ascent for it, so this is its stated lower bound; re-flying idea 35 with the lighter payload drops AS-512's flight-report plan and idea 36's guidance does not settle).
- *The closed loop:* for `-sc` rockets the spacecraft is sized first (ideas 39 to 42 with no flown departure), the rocket flown with that payload, then 39 to 42 again from the departure it produced, until the payload and idea 42's launch mass agree to 0.01 kg (AS-512: 2,141.27, then 2,262.93, then 2,262.93 kg).
- *The aim home (added in review):* idea 47 aims every return at a 125 km perigee (its default), which for the skip entry idea 48 chooses is a flyby; idea 48 says so in its `retarget` refusal and names the perigee to aim at. For `-sc` rockets the runner does what that refusal asks: idea 47 flown again with `aimAlt_m` at idea 48's perigee, then 48 and 49, until idea 48 needs no re-aim (AS-512 to Apophis: one pass, 56.431 km asked, 55.089 km flown). The as-flown rockets and the committed chain keep idea 47's default aim.
- *Asteroid:* Apophis takes Aster's own cell through idea 39's defaults (the chain's); any other body is solved by idea 39 first (least delta-v for a sample return, `dv_return`, over its default 2026-01-01 to 2027-07-01 window) and that transfer's departure v-infinity and date are handed to idea 37 as its asymptote.
- *A halt:* any module that refuses stops the chain there, and the refusal is the mission's. Since the review that includes idea 37 handing on no launch (its guidance refusing the target): the chain stops at 37 with that refusal, not at 38 with a missing-output error.

**What each link is reduced to** (`mission.js` `EXTRACT`): about 15 numbers per module, each read from one path of its handoff with that output's confidence class (`measured`/`derived`/`assumed`), and the module's own refusals. The page names the path of every number it shows.

**The mission** (`mission()`): the events in time order (TDB seconds past J2000, UTC beside them as each module gave them; TDB − UTC = 69.184 s, Aster's offset, as every module uses), the masses at them, the delta-v burns with who pays and whether the link says they flew, the durations (launch to landing), the refusals, the seams and the verdict.
- *Refusal flow* (`refusalFlow()`): for each link, the refusals the link before it carried or raised, by `from:applies_to`, against the ones it carries on. A missing one is *answered* when the module says it answers it in its README (idea 35 answers 34's `losses`; 36 answers 35's `guidance`, `orbit` and `trajectory`; 40 lifts 39's `dynamics` and 38's `post_cutoff`; a test quotes each README) and *dropped* otherwise.
- *Seams* (`seams()`): `departure` (breaks: idea 38's stage runs dry; everything after flies a departure that did not happen), `tag_propellant` (breaks: idea 42 sizes the tanks without idea 44's touch-and-go), `retarget` (breaks, added in review: idea 47's aim is a perigee where idea 48's entry does not work, and the re-aim's propellant is not aboard), `payload` (the rocket's payload against idea 42's spacecraft), `payload_swap` (idea 38's payload setting against the payload the ascent carried), `departure_epoch` (idea 40's cruise against the departure point idea 37 put the burn at), `ascent_refly` and `arrival_vinf` (notes: idea 37 re-flies idea 36's ascent on the window's azimuth; idea 40's flown arrival speed against idea 39's conic), `plant_mass` (idea 46's coolers appear at the asteroid without having been launched), `container` (the material rides home with no structure or heat shield booked), `entry_mass`, `comms` (idea 41's link plan against the stay flown and the return leg), `heat_shield` (idea 49's thickness refused), `refusals_dropped`.
- *Verdict:* closes when idea 49 lands, nothing halted, every link from the rocket to the capsule is present (34 to 49, and 31 to 33 unless the vehicle is AS-512, whose engines are idea 33's presets) and no seam breaks. Blocking refusals: `departure_propellant`, `chain_departure`, `payload_capability`, `tankage`, `parachute_sequence`, `return`, `landing` and (review) `retarget`, unless a later module answers them.

**The what-if** (`create().closure()`), every other link held:
- *Out:* idea 42's sizing, propellant = dry × (e^(Δv/vₑ) − 1) with vₑ = Isp × 9.80665 m/s² (idea 42's G0, `closing.js:28`) and Δv its TCMs + approach burns + station-keeping (+ idea 44's touch-and-go Δv when that box is ticked); the arrival and stand-off masses through the same burns; the touch-and-go priced on the stand-off mass as idea 44 prices it.
- *The launch:* the flown rocket's capability is idea 38's `payload_capability.finite_burn_kg` (a lower bound: the ascent held); a published launcher is Aster's `payloadAtC3` (transcribed from `launch-vehicles.ts`, exact at an anchor, linear between, refused off the curve, Aster's nine golden pins reproduced) at idea 39's C3. Margin = capability − launch mass.
- *Home:* idea 47's `ledger()`, `materialMax()` and `capacity()` (`home.js` lines 185-218, copied verbatim into `snapshots/home-ledger.js`; a test checks the block is a byte-for-byte substring) with idea 47's burns, wait and flight time, idea 46's propellant and the dry mass the chain uses (the stand-off mass ideas 44 to 46 carry, plus idea 46's coolers unless they were launched).
- *Entry and landing:* idea 49's ablated mass held; the main parachute 2 m g₀ / (ρ v²) at the ground (`hotreturn.js:344`) with PartForge's 1976 atmosphere `isa()` (idea 49's copy, byte-identical), and its equivalent in OSIRIS-REx mains from idea 49's own ratio.
- *The re-aim (review):* the what-if holds idea 47's aim, so where idea 48 needs a re-aim it has no propellant for, the what-if says so and does not close. Booking it in idea 47's ledger instead is no way out on the chain's numbers: with the 79.8 mm/s at the Earth the craft cannot come home even empty, because the 0.80 kg of hydrogen made boils off on the way (0.0104 kg/day).
- Stated on the page: the water made and so the propellant are held (the dig was limited by its oven, not the spacecraft's weight, on the chain), and so is idea 49's ablation.

**NHATS** (`create().roundTrip()`). JPL's definition, quoted from CNEOS's caveats page: "The total delta-V includes the Earth departure maneuver from a 400 km altitude circular parking orbit, the maneuver to match the NEA's velocity at arrival, the maneuver to depart the NEA and, if necessary, a maneuver to control the atmospheric re-entry speed during Earth return." The Earth departure is Aster's `injectionDvFromC3` with Aster's μ (398,600.4418 km³/s², `delta-v.ts:3`) at Aster's Earth radius (6,378,136.6 m, `bodies.ts:45`) + 400 km; braking lowers v∞ to what gives 12 km/s at 125 km (NHATS's limit, quoted). The legs: idea 39's `cell()` at NHATS's launch date, 0 h TDB (as idea 47 reads NHATS's dates), for the outbound time (C3 and arrival v∞); idea 47's own `nhatsChecks()` for the departure burn and the Earth-arrival v∞ of the same trajectory id. A row is *outside idea 39's envelope* when idea 39's own `encounters()` finds an Earth encounter between the target's element epoch and the arrival (`lambert.js:403, 423`: its grid refuses such cells).

**Data, all copied or fetched, none typed.** `snapshots/aster-*.txt`: Aster's `delta-v.ts`, `launch-vehicles.ts`, `bodies.ts` and its launch-vehicle golden test at `62ddb17`, byte-for-byte; `aster.js` parsed from them (8 vehicles, 38 anchors, the count checked against the golden test's own). `nhats.js`: the 43 NHATS API queries behind idea 47's rows (61 trajectories, each with every term), fetched 2026-11-01, and eight phrases found verbatim in CNEOS's caveats page and the API documentation (re-fetched in review: both pages hash to the recorded sha256). `isa.js` and `home-ledger.js`: ideas 49 and 47, verbatim. `missions.js`: every mission, run by `run-chain.mjs --build`, packed (links and refusals shared; numbers to 10 significant figures), with the git blob ids of the 228 files of ideas 31 to 49 it loaded rolled into one sha256. `nhats-chain.js`: the chain's two legs on each NHATS trajectory.

**Handoff schema (version 1, `from: "50-the-long-way-round"`).** `handoff.json` is the sample (the chain's own choices, this page's default).

| inputs | |
|---|---|
| `choice` | `{rocket, propellant, asteroid}` (`imported` for a walked import) |
| `source` | `bundled`, `imported` or `run-chain.mjs` |
| `what_if` | the what-if's settings `{dry, plant, tag, launcher, td}` (null where the chain's) |
| `record` | the mission's links, packed (`packRecords()` of one record: each link's extracted numbers in `EXTRACT` order, confidences, own refusals, digest, bytes and carried count; the halt, the closed loop, the aim home, the notes and the refusal flow) |

| outputs | units | confidence |
|---|---|---|
| `verdict` `{closes, lands, halted_at, missing, blockers [{id, between, what}], summary}` | | derived |
| `timeline` `[{event, tdb_s_past_j2000, utc, from}]` | s (TDB past J2000), UTC | derived |
| `mass_ledger` `[{event, mass_kg, from}]` | kg | derived |
| `delta_v_budget` `[{burn, dv_m_s, payer, flown, from, note}]` | m/s | derived |
| `durations` `{launch_to_cruise_s, cruise_s, stay_s, return_s, total_s}` | s | derived |
| `seams` `[{id, severity, between, what, a, b, jump, units, what_would_help}]` | per seam | derived |
| `links` `[{n, from, produced, sha256, bytes, own_refusals, carried_refusals}]` | | measured |
| `closure` (the what-if, when exported from the page) `{outbound, launch, home, entry, refusals, assumptions, closes}` | kg, m/s, m², m²/s² | derived |

`refusals[]`: every link's own refusals with their `from` (and `answered_by` where a later module answers one), a halt's refusal, and this module's own `mission` refusal when the mission does not close. Frames: this module reads magnitudes, masses and epochs, each in the frame its module states. **Reading one back** (`parseHandoff`): the record is unpacked, the mission made again and every output compared exactly (the outputs are made from the packed record, so a read-back re-derives them to the bit), and the refusal codes compared; an edited verdict, mass, link digest or dropped refusal is refused. Since the review the what-if is re-run from its own settings and compared to 1e-8 wherever the reader has the snapshots (the page and the tests do; `parseHandoff` without them checks everything else). A handoff of any of ideas 31 to 49 is walked down its inputs; a link carried as a digest (ideas 45 to 49 prune their predecessor to the sha256 of its `JSON.stringify`) is resolved against every mission in the bundle (the committed chain by its full digest, each flown mission by the 16-hex prefix the bundle keeps), with that mission's own upstream, so idea 49's handoff from any flown mission walks whole. A chain that still has links missing is never called closed.
