Stack Ledger

A gap as a signed chain of contributors, added up the only way that gates: worst case. Each contributor is an ISO 286 fit from PartForge's fits.json, an as-supplied surface from stock.json, a differential expansion from materials_thermal.json through the shipped dbFitThermal(), a declared dimension with its tol_src, or a coating build-up. RSS is a footnote, centred on the mid-limit means, with its N. What the tables refuse, the page refuses, and prints no number in its place.

    Contributors

    #dircontributorlowhighsignedfrom

    Limits are absolute (mm). signed is the contributor's band after its direction: the worst-case gap is the sum of the signed lows and the sum of the signed highs. Confidence words are the tables' own: likely or recall for a table row (a recall row is never presented as cited), source / user / default for a declaration (PartForge's tol_src).

    Stack JSON (edit it; the ledger follows)

    Kinds: declared (nominal_mm, plus_mm/minus_mm or tol_mm, tol_src, tol_ref, dir), fit (size_mm, hole ["H",7], shaft ["g",6]), iso (size_mm, feature, letter, grade, dir), stock (key, surface od|id|thickness, dir), coating (per_side_mm or [min,max], surfaces, src), thermal (D_mm, hole_mat, shaft_mat, dT_K [low,high], hoop_cte). dir is +1 when the contributor opens the gap and −1 when it closes it. Units are mm.
