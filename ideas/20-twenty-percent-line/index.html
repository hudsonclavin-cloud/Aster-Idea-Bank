Twenty percent line

Skyhook's construction baseline is an HMPE arm with a Zylon (PBO) tip. Its sizing rule loads the HMPE to a fixed fraction of break while a payload rides; published guidance for multi-year creep life is lower. This page sizes the arm three ways and says, in tonnes, what keeping HMPE under the line costs, and where along the arm the extra PBO should go.
