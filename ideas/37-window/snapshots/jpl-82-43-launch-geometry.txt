Notation: phi_L launch-site geocentric latitude, lambda_L its east longitude; alpha_L the site's right ascension at
liftoff; delta_inf (DLA), alpha_inf (RLA) the outgoing asymptote's declination and right ascension; Sigma_L the launch
azimuth "measured in the site's local horizontal plane, clockwise from north"; i, Omega the parking orbit's
inclination and ascending node; theta the range angle from the launch site to the asymptote, in the trajectory plane.
