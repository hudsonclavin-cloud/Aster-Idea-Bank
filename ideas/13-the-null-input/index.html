The Null Input

Skyhook's params/rev_a.json carries economics.fiber_cost_per_kg: null, its weakest input, and the finance model prices fiber launch, not fiber purchase. This page re-derives that model from the workbook ladder, proves it at $0/kg against the published figures, then adds the missing line and lets you type the quote.

The sweep

Every price here is hypothetical. The purchase line is $/kg × multiplier × fiber kg, added to total capex and spread 15/30/35/20 % over years 1–4 like every other capex dollar in the workbook; the hardware mass factor is not applied to it (it scales launched mass, not fiber bought). Option-tier rows are the partial tier model: only tip speed is tier-specific.

How much each conservative lever must move to close the case, as the fiber price rises

Relaxation required = |threshold − conservative value| ÷ conservative value, for each input alone (bisection as finance/sensitivity.py). A line that leaves the chart means no value within a 5× stretch closes the case. Dotted verticals are the repository's markers. Fixed annual ops cannot close the case at any price and is not drawn.

Which baseline-value singles still close the conservative case

Each bar is one conservative input swapped alone to its baseline-column value (SENSITIVITY_FINDINGS §2); the bar ends at the fiber price where that single stops bringing payback inside Y15. Markers again dotted.
