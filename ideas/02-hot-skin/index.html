Hot Skin

The stagnation and recovery temperature of air at a speed and altitude are an upper bound on how hot a part's skin can get, not a prediction of how hot it does get. This page measures that bound against each PartForge flight material's service_max_c. The verdict is SCREEN, never a pass.

    Margins in °C: positive means the bound sits under the limit. over: even the turbulent recovery temperature exceeds the limit. stagnation: only stagnation points (leading edge, nose tip) exceed it. near: the stagnation bound is within 25 °C of the limit. under: the bound is below the limit; the limit itself is still judgement, not a rating. Confidence: likely = nominal, agreeing across handbooks; recall = model recall, not cited.
