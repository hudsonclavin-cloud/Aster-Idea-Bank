1 The declaration

Source, hash, counts and tolerance provenance
The FLIGHT block this ledger is bound to

Its sha256 is taken over exactly these lines, line endings normalised to LF. For a file with one block it is what sed -n '/FLIGHT-BEGIN/,/FLIGHT-END/p' part.scad | sha256sum prints. Any edit inside the block, even one digit, makes a different declaration; edits elsewhere in the file do not.

        How the verdicts are made
        
    
          
      
        • IN: every reading lies within nominal ± tolerance, limits included. Not the mean: an oval OD read 146.14 and 146.26 against 146.20 ± 0.05 has a perfect mean and is OUT. PartForge's own mesh check gates the same way, on the smallest and largest size it finds (cmmVerdicts(), index.html:3849). Compared with no allowance for the instrument.
          • 
      
        • OUT: at least one reading is outside. One OUT makes the part NONCONFORMING however much is still unmeasured.
          • 
      
        • UNDECIDED: GO/NO-GO members that cannot settle it, because one is missing or sits outside the band. A pin that enters proves the hole is at least its size; a ring that passes over proves the shaft is at most its size.
          • 
      
        • NOT MEASURED: no entry, or an entry with something missing: no instrument, no resolution, a number that does not parse. No verdict is guessed.
          • 
      
        • Sizes are compared exactly, in decimal: 6.60 + 0.10 is 6.699999999999999 in floating point, and a 6.70 hole is inside a 6.60 ± 0.10 band. Inches convert at exactly 25.4 mm; inch limits are printed rounded inward.
          • 
      
        • 10:1 is a shop rule of thumb, not a cited standard: an instrument whose resolution (or stated accuracy, if larger) is more than a tenth of the tolerance band is flagged. So is any reading within that figure of a limit, and any reading that is not a step of the stated resolution.
          • 
      
        • The declaration is read with PartForge's own parseFlight() (index.html:4672, copied verbatim at 0974a62), and a report through its own measurementRows() (headless/partforge.mjs:97). The page never relaxes or proposes a tolerance; a tolerance the tool proposed is labelled tool default wherever it appears.
